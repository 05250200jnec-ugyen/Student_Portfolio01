About Me

Hello! I'm Ugyen Dorji, a student of Computer System and Network (CSN) at Jigme Namgyel Engineering College, Dewathang, under the Royal University of Bhutan. I enjoy understanding how data travels from one device to another and how software can make that process faster, safer and easier to manage.

My main strengths are networking and coding. I like setting up and troubleshooting networks in Cisco Packet Tracer, configuring routing and switching, and writing programs in Python, C and JavaScript. I learn best by building things, so most of what I know comes from hands-on labs and personal projects.

Career goal: I want to become a skilled Network and Systems Engineer who can build and secure digital infrastructure for organisations in Bhutan, and contribute to the country's growing ICT sector. I am also working towards industry certifications such as CCNA.
